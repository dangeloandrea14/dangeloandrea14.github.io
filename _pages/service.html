--- layout: archive title: "Service" permalink: /service/ author_profile: true --- {% include base_path %} {% comment %} ============================================================================= SERVICE — "The Record" The page used to render every commitment at the same weight, so a proceedings chairship read exactly like a workshop committee seat, and the reviewing history was eleven rows of "years / conference" that nobody parses. Two moves fix that: 1. ORGANISING is a weighted list. A year rail down the left, the title in Newsreader, and a chip on the right that names the contribution — "Proceedings Chair" for the two chairships, "Workshop / Tutorial / Session / Event Organization" for the rest, so the chip alone says the event was run, not attended. The chip label is derived: a role naming a Proceedings Chair wins, otherwise it comes from the entry's `type`. Within a year the chairships are listed first — the date alone would bury a proceedings chairship under workshops that fall later that year. 2. REVIEWING is a matrix, not a list. One row per venue, one column per year in the span, a filled cell where the seat was served — so three years running on the same programme committee reads as a horizontal run. The Distinguished Reviewer cell is gold rather than a parenthesis. The matrix and the journal list come from _data/service.yml; the organising list comes from the _service collection. The year columns are derived from the data, so adding a 2028 seat grows the matrix on its own. Everything here is rendered server-side: there is no JavaScript on this page. ============================================================================= {% endcomment %} {% assign by_year = site.service | group_by_exp: "s", "s.date | date: '%Y'" | sort: "name" | reverse %}
