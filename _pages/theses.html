Two things carry the state: 1. a status rail down the left of every row — open topics get the green rail, completed ones a quiet grey, so what is available reads at a glance without anyone having to parse a badge; 2. a status filter — All / Available / Completed — over a single list. "All" is the default: open topics first, completed ones greyed out beneath them, nothing hidden until someone asks for it. Columns are fixed: Topic, University, Year. A row carries a university whenever its `university_key` resolves in _data/universities.yml, so open topics can name a host institution too — set the key and the column fills. A title that has body text behind it is a button: it opens the text in place, under the title, rather than sending anyone to a near-empty stub page. Titles with nothing to show are plain text.
